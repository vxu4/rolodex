HOW DO WE FEEL ABOUT STICKERS?

In Korea, I kept thinking about our conversation; about islands as crystals, contours of islands, artificial stones, pouring cement to make lands, and stickers. I like the idea of stickers more and more, because stickers are just like islands with their contours and floating bodies.

With some image collecting I made some drawings of islands, like Lantau Island, Jeju Island, and Long Island, with patterns of stones, as if sliced at the horizon. They are not accurate in shape or true to scale and ratio. I would like for them to look like random stones that are sliced rather than recognizable island geography.
